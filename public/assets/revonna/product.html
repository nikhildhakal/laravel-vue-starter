About Our Organic Raw Honey

Our raw honey is harvested directly from the hive, strained to remove debris, and bottled without any pasteurization or filtering. This preserves all the natural enzymes, antioxidants, and phytonutrients that make raw honey a true superfood.

Sourced from local organic beekeepers who practice sustainable beekeeping methods, our honey comes from bees that forage on wildflowers, giving it a unique, complex flavor profile that changes slightly with each batch.

  • Never heated above 105°F to preserve nutrients
  • No additives or preservatives
  • Rich in antioxidants and enzymes
  • Naturally antibacterial and antifungal
  • Great for sweetening teas, baking, or direct consumption

Store your honey at room temperature in a cool, dark place. Avoid refrigeration as it can cause crystallization. If your honey does crystallize (a natural process for raw honey), simply place the jar in warm water to liquefy it again.

While honey is a natural product made by bees, some vegans choose not to consume it as it's an animal byproduct. Our honey is harvested using sustainable beekeeping practices that prioritize bee health and welfare.
